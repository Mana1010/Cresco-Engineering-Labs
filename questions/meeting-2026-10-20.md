3. Fixtures are re-imported on every migrate and overwrite edits. Is that acceptable for sample data, or should sample data be a one-time seed (inserted only if missing)?
4. "Bitmojis" on the Home page: does this mean the avatars, such as the birthday avatars on the `/portal` dashboard? Engineering Labs Home itself has no avatars.

**Tools vs calculations**

5. What's the difference between a **tool** and a **calculation**? My current guess, to confirm:
   - A **tool** (Tools Registry) is a reusable calculator like CheckWind.
   - A **calculation** (Calculation Catalog) is one saved run of a tool for a project, which then goes through approval.

   If that's right, the data needs two DocTypes, Tool and Calculation; today there's one combined "Labs Calculation". Mockup 6 shows tools such as "Lintel Loading Calculator v1.0" awaiting approval, so do **tools** also need approval, or only calculation runs?

**Permissions**

6. Which roles exist in portal_core, and what can each do? For example, who sees which tools, who submits, and who approves or deprecates? Once DocType permissions use those roles, the API can switch to `frappe.get_list` and normal `save()`, as the notes require.

**Runner page**

7. Should the runner be its own page (e.g. `/engineering-labs/tools/<tool>`) rather than a modal? Should "Run" and "Add to Project" be two separate buttons (run to see the result, then add it to the project), or one button that does both?
8. With blank inputs, should anything be shown before a project is chosen, such as disabled fields? After choosing a project, which fields auto-fill for CheckWind besides site address?
